Step-by-Step Matrix Arithmetic

The forward pass, one dot product at a time

How does the prompt <BOS><CHAR=BLUEY><MODE=BEDTIME><PLACE=BEDROOM><THEME=HELP> become the next token? The forward pass of StoryLM's block is the same everywhere - only the sizes change. Here it is at four dimensions (d_model = 4, 2 heads of 2, V = 8, T = 5), so every multiply, rotation and softmax can be checked by hand. The weights are not invented: they are four of the real checkpoint's 128 dimensions (block 1 of 8, dimensions 103-106), and one block on them already predicts what the full model predicts.

Compare: Toy Walkthrough vs. Real StoryLM Dimensions

Attention Heads ($h$) 2 heads ($d_{\text{head}} = 2$) 8 heads ($d_{\text{head}} = 16$) Two heads of two dimensions, each with its own RoPE pair and its own softmax, concatenated afterwards - the real mechanics, not merged.

Depth (blocks) 1 block walked (8 also measured) 8 blocks Steps 1-7 are one block. The exporter also runs all eight truncated blocks: same prediction.
